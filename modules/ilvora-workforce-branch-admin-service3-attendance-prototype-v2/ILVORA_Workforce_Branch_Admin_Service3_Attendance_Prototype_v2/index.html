Attendance Marker / Kiosk

Integrated permission
Attendance Operator permission: authorised marker can assist check-in/check-out and verify employee identity. This does not grant HR access or permission to edit punches, approve corrections or manage employee records.
QR, OTP, face verification and GPS are shown as workflow options only. This prototype does not access a camera, GPS, send OTPs, validate a live rotating QR code or write to a production attendance database.

Marker permissions

  • Can assist with employee check-in/check-out using approved verification methods.
  • Can view punch confirmation for the current transaction.
  • Cannot edit or delete punches, approve corrections, view bank/payroll data or access unrelated HR records.
  • Any correction must follow the Branch Admin/HR → Head Office approval workflow.

Submit formal correction to Head Office

Branch Admin/HR may submit but cannot approve the correction. Attendance Marker cannot submit a formal correction. The requester cannot approve their own request. If payroll has closed, an approved change must trigger a payroll adjustment, not a silent historical edit.

Head Office decision (read-only at this role)

Head Office Admin reviews the original punch, request, evidence and audit trail, then approves, rejects or returns the request. On approval, the system updates attendance while preserving the original punch and notifies the employee and Branch Admin/HR.

Attendance reports & period closure

Payroll attendance closure

Attendance register closes after the 20th of the month as per the configured payroll cycle. Branch Admin/HR reviews and submits branch exceptions before closure. Corrections approved after payroll closure must be handled as payroll adjustments.
  • Branch Admin/HR reviews branch attendance and unresolved exceptions.
  • Submit formal correction requests to Head Office before final closure where possible.
  • Preserve attendance summaries, approvals and audit evidence in accordance with the retention schedule.
  • Printing and physical filing must be recorded in the print/filing register.

Attendance Record Retention & Due-Date Notifications

Monthly controls
Policy: For each attendance month, the detailed attendance register must be printed by the 15th of the following month. The detailed digital report is scheduled for deletion after the 21st of the following month. A brief attendance summary is retained for 3 years and then automatically deleted, subject to legal holds and approved retention requirements.

Notification rules

  • Print reminder starts 5 calendar days before the 15th deadline (from the 10th) and continues until the print/filing register confirms completion.
  • Detailed-report deletion reminder starts 5 calendar days before the 21st (from the 16th) and continues until deletion is completed or an authorised hold is recorded.
  • Brief-summary expiry reminders start 5 days before its individual 3-year expiry date.
  • Notify the responsible Branch Admin/HR and Head Office records custodian. Escalate overdue printing or deletion to Head Office.
  • Notifications must show attendance month, due date, action required and current completion/hold status.

Monthly record processing

Legal hold safeguard: Automatic deletion must pause for litigation, investigation, audit, regulatory requirement or other authorised legal hold. A retention rule must never delete a record while a valid hold is active. The physical printed register follows its separately approved physical-record retention and secure-destruction schedule; the 3-year brief-summary rule does not automatically apply to printed detailed records.

Prototype only: reminders and deletion dates are displayed as policy workflows. This page does not send notifications or delete production records. Production needs server-side scheduled jobs, notification delivery, deletion logs, legal-hold controls and verified print/filing confirmation.
