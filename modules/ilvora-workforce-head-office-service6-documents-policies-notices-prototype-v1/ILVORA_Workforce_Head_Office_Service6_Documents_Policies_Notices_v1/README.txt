ILVORA WORKFORCE
Head Office Admin Panel – Service 6: Documents, Policies & Notices
Prototype v1

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
3. Explore Policies & Documents, Upload & Publish, Notice Management, Employee Delivery & Reads, and Audit & Retention.
4. Try uploading a sample file, publishing a common or branch-specific policy, and publishing/withdrawing a notice.
5. Select “Branch Admin / HR – submit for approval” to see the notice approval workflow.

INCLUDED
- Upload interface for PDF, DOC/DOCX and image files (demo records selected filename only).
- Common policy publishing for all branches or branch-specific publishing.
- Policy metadata, versions, issue/effective/expiry dates and mandatory acknowledgement setting.
- Employee document/policy register and simulated view.
- Automatic notification workflow records for affected employees.
- Employee Notice management for common, branch-specific and individual employee audiences.
- Branch Admin / HR notice submissions routed for higher-level approval.
- Head Office approval/rejection, higher-level publishing and withdrawal/removal controls.
- Notification delivery/read/acknowledgement register.
- Audit history, CSV export and print/Save as PDF.
- Retention and legal-hold guidance.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
This is a front-end demonstration only. It does not connect to Cloudflare D1, GitHub, live employee records, real authentication, role permissions, file storage, SMS gateways or email providers. All records and actions are sample data held in the browser session.
The prototype does not actually send SMS or email. It creates illustrative notification queue entries. Production requires secure server-side storage, employee-to-branch targeting, an SMS gateway, email service, delivery webhooks, retry handling, access controls and immutable audit logging.
File upload captures the selected filename only; no file bytes are saved. Production must use private object storage, malware scanning, file type/size validation, encryption and authorised access.
A notice withdrawn from the active employee board must remain in the audit record. Policy versions and acknowledgements must not be overwritten. Final retention periods require HR/legal review and legal/audit/investigation/regulatory holds.
