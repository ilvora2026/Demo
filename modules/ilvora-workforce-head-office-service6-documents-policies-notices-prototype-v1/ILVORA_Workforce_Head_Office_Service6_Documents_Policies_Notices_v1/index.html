Employees can read or download published documents applicable to their branch or all branches. Previous versions remain in the controlled history.

Publishing controls

Common publishing: one approved policy can be made available to employees in all three branches.
Branch publishing: restricts visibility and notifications to active employees assigned to the selected branch.
Mandatory acknowledgement: enable when employees must confirm they have read the policy.
Version control: new versions should supersede, not overwrite, prior published versions.

Upload a policy or employee document

Prototype captures the selected filename only. Production requires secure file upload and virus scanning.

Publication workflow

  1. Upload and enter document metadata.
  2. Select common or one branch as the audience.
  3. Confirm issue/effective dates and acknowledgement requirement.
  4. Publish using an authorised role.
  5. System creates employee tasks and sends notifications to registered mobile and email contacts.
  6. Track delivery, reads and acknowledgements.
In production, policy publication should be permission-checked and logged with publisher, timestamp, version, audience and file checksum.

Create and manage employee notices

Notice authority and control

Branch Admin / HR: can create and submit a notice for their own branch. It remains pending until approved by the authorised higher-level role.
Head Office Admin: can issue approved common notices and branch-specific notices within delegated authority.
Administrator / Managing Director: higher-level authority can publish, withdraw or remove active notices according to assigned permissions.
Withdrawal: removes a notice from the active employee notice board, but the audit record and publication history remain.

Automatic notification rules

  • New policy published → notify all active employees in the selected audience.
  • Common policy/notice → all three branches.
  • Branch policy/notice → only active employees assigned to that branch.
  • Individual notice → only the selected employee ID.
  • Mandatory policy → track acknowledgement and issue reminders to pending employees.
  • Keep in-app notice available even if SMS or email delivery fails.
SMS and email require configured production providers. A failed external notification must not erase the in-app item or its delivery failure record.

Audit history

Retention and records controls

  • Retain published policy versions and their approval/publication history according to the legally approved corporate records schedule.
  • Retain acknowledgement evidence with employee ID, policy version, timestamp and method.
  • Retain notice issue, approval, delivery, withdrawal and read history according to the approved schedule.
  • Apply legal, audit, investigation and regulatory holds before deletion.
  • Maintain physical print/filing records for documents designated for physical retention.
Do not automatically delete policies, medical/safety records or acknowledgement evidence merely because a new version is published. Final retention periods must be confirmed by HR/legal review.
Access should follow least privilege. Sensitive employee documents must be restricted to authorised roles and never exposed through a public document URL.
