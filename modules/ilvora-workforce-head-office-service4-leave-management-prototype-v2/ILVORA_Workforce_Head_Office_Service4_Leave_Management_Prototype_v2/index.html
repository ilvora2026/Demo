Leave approval authority

Paid medical leave: Head Office approval is mandatory for every paid medical-leave request.
Annual paid medical limit: Maximum 5 approved paid medical-leave days per employee per year. Additional medical leave is unpaid.
Casual leave: Unpaid. Branch Admin / HR may approve eligible requests up to 5 days under delegated authority.
Escalation: Any leave request exceeding 5 days is routed to Head Office.

Decision checklist

  • Employee and branch identity verified
  • Dates and duration checked
  • Supporting documents reviewed where required
  • Annual paid medical leave balance checked
  • Paid/unpaid classification recorded
  • Decision reason and approver retained
Document retention after printing: Temporary or duplicate uploads may be deleted after a successful print and filing confirmation. Medical certificates and legally required leave evidence must be retained digitally for 5 years, subject to legal review. Printing does not authorise deletion of these retained records. Legal, audit or investigation holds pause deletion.
Physical record control: Record the print date, printed-by user, application/report reference, page count and physical filing location in the print register. Store printed records securely with access restricted to authorised staff.
Prototype limitation: Sample data and browser-only decisions. No live leave balance, attendance, payroll, document storage, notifications, authentication, print verification or scheduled deletion is connected. Production requires server-side controls, secure storage, audit logs and scheduled retention jobs.
