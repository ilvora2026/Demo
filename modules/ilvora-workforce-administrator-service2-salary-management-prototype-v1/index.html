Production controls required

  • Server-side role-based access and least privilege.
  • Independent approval for salary changes; requester must not approve their own change.
  • Effective-dated salary history; never overwrite old salary records.
  • Payroll must use only approved salary versions effective for the pay period.
  • Encrypted storage, audit logs, access monitoring and masked exports.
  • Configure statutory deductions and legal review before go-live.
