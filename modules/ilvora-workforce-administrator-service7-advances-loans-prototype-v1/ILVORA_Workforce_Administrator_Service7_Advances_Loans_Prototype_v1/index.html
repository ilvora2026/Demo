Approval & Repayment Controls

Administrator authority

May approve requests at or below 25% of the employee’s monthly salary, after eligibility and outstanding-balance checks.

Managing Director authority

Sole approval authority for requests above 25%. Administrator cannot override or approve these requests.

Branch Admin / HR

May verify employment details, salary record reference and prior advances, then recommend. No approval or disbursement rights.

Repayment tracking

Record approved amount, disbursement date, instalment amount, deductions, paid instalments and outstanding balance. Payroll deduction starts only after approval and recorded disbursement.

Mandatory controls: employee application and consent; eligibility check; outstanding advance check; approval by the correct authority; recorded disbursement; repayment schedule; employee-visible balance; audit trail. Do not deduct from salary without documented authorisation and applicable legal review.
