Upload and submit employee document

Choose an existing employee. Upload a PDF or image and route it to Head Office for verification.
Workflow
  1. Branch Admin selects employee and document category.
  2. Uploads document and submits for verification.
  3. Head Office checks authenticity and completeness.
  4. Head Office verifies, rejects or requests clarification.
  5. Decision and reviewer details are recorded in audit history.
Privacy: Store only necessary documents, restrict access by role and encrypt files at rest and in transit.
