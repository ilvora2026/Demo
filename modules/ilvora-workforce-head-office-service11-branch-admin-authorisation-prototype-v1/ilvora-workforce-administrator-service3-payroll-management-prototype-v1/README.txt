ILVORA Workforce – Administrator Service 3
Payroll Management Prototype v1

RUN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. Explore Payroll Run, Review & Exceptions, Payroll Register, Payslips, History and Controls.

FEATURES
- Monthly payroll preview with branch scope and source-readiness checks.
- Sample gross, allowance, leave/attendance adjustment, deductions and net totals.
- Exception review queue.
- Branch-wise payroll register, employee search and CSV export.
- Individual sample payslip preview and print/Save as PDF through browser.
- Payroll history concept, authority boundaries and monthly schedule.
- Payroll close and audit-control checklist.

LIMITATIONS / SAFETY
- Front-end demo only. Employee names, IDs and values are fictional.
- No live login, server, database, attendance/leave/salary integration, statutory calculation,
  payroll approval, bank transfer, payslip email, audit service or real access enforcement.
- Demo interactions do not persist after refresh. Do not enter real employee or payroll data.
- Payroll calculations are illustrative and are not legally or financially reliable.
- Production requires qualified payroll/legal review, statutory deduction configuration,
  secure server-side role permissions, independent approval, immutable audit logs,
  encryption, retention controls and bank-file validation.

GOVERNANCE
- Administrator prepares and approves payroll; preparer must not approve their own run.
- Only approved effective-dated salary records, verified attendance and approved leave
  should feed payroll.
- Closed payroll is locked; corrections must be posted as adjustment entries in a later run.
- Payslips should be delivered via authenticated, expiring access rather than exposed files.
- Maintain digital records and physical print/filing register under an approved retention schedule.
