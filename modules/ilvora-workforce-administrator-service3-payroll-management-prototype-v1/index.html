Monthly schedule

  • Attendance register closes after the 20th.
  • Payroll review and processing before the 12th payment target for the applicable cycle.
  • Salary payment target: before the 12th.
  • Payslip generation: 12th of each month.

Payroll close controls

  • Use only approved salary versions effective for the payroll period.
  • Use approved leave and verified attendance records.
  • Flag missing punches, pending leave and unapproved salary revisions.
  • Require independent review; preparer cannot approve their own payroll.
  • Lock approved payroll and preserve immutable calculation snapshot.
  • Post-close corrections must use adjustment entries in a later run.
  • Maintain digital audit trail and physical print/filing register.
  • Apply statutory rules, tax, PF/ESI and other deductions only after qualified payroll/legal review.
