ILVORA Workforce – Head Office Admin Panel
Service 1: Head Office Dashboard – Prototype v1

Open index.html in a modern browser.

Includes:
- Central dashboard across the three configured branches
- Workforce and attendance KPIs
- Branch-wise workforce snapshot and attendance comparison
- Central approval queue
- Compliance and escalation alerts
- Navigation concept for Head Office services
- Print dashboard

All values are illustrative. This is a front-end prototype only, not connected to Cloudflare D1 or live employee data. Production requires authentication, server-side role and branch permissions, audited workflows, secure data access, and testing. Dashboard visibility does not itself grant approval or editing permissions.
