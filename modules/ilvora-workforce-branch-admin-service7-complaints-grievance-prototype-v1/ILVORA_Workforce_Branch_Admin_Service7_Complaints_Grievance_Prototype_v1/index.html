Every complaint is linked to an employee ID. Anonymous submissions are not accepted. Confidentiality requests are recorded and access is restricted.
Confidentiality and independent routing
  • Complaint is linked to the submitting employee ID.
  • If Branch Admin / HR is named or implicated, the case bypasses that person and routes to an independent authorised Head Office officer.
  • If Head Office HR or senior management is implicated, route to a designated independent authority / external channel under company policy.
  • Only authorised handlers may see complaint details and attachments.
  • Do not disclose complainant identity beyond those who need it to investigate, subject to fair process and applicable law.
Urgent safety risk: Follow the organisation's immediate safety and emergency escalation procedure; this register is not an emergency response channel.
