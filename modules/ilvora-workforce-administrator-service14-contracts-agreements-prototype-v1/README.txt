ILVORA Workforce — Administrator Service 14
Contracts & Agreement Management
Version 1 — Front-end demonstration

RUN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or another modern browser.

FEATURES
- Central contract and agreement register across all three branches
- Building/premises leases, vendor agreements, equipment maintenance, software/IT, consultancy and other services
- Contract parties, start/end dates, value, payment frequency, owner, notice period and key terms
- Document reference capture and version/lifecycle workflow concepts
- Obligation and renewal tracker with 90/60/30-day reminder concept
- Approval, amendment, renewal, termination and dispute workflow concepts
- Branch-wise and consolidated report catalogue, CSV export and print/Save PDF
- Role boundaries and production control checklist

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
This is a browser-based prototype using illustrative sample records. It is not connected to Cloudflare D1, GitHub, authentication, email/SMS, e-signature, legal services, accounting or payment systems. It does not upload or securely store documents; it records a filename/reference only. Data entered during the demo is not persisted to a backend. Approval buttons are illustrative and do not execute real approvals.

AUTHORITY GOVERNANCE
- Branch Admin/HR may submit agreement details and supporting documents.
- Administrator maintains the register and monitors obligations; may approve only within an MD-delegated limit.
- Managing Director approves above-limit and reserved matters, sets limits and defines authorised signatories.
- No numerical contract approval limit was provided. Until the MD formally sets an effective-dated limit and signatory matrix, route new agreements, financially binding renewals, material amendments and termination settlements to the MD.
- Requester must not approve their own request. Do not split related commitments to bypass approval limits.

PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS
Implement server-side RBAC, private encrypted document storage, malware scanning, immutable audit logs, effective-dated approval/signatory limits, secure notifications with delivery logs, version control, conflict declarations, legal/finance review, renewal escalation, backup/restore, and a formally approved India/Kerala legal, tax, financial and sector-specific retention schedule. Obtain qualified legal advice for contract templates, execution, stamp duty, registration and enforceability as applicable.
