Contract lifecycle checklist

  1. Request and business justification
  2. Counterparty due diligence and conflict declaration
  3. Legal / finance / operational review as applicable
  4. Approval under delegated authority
  5. Execution by authorised signatory
  6. Register signed version and obligations
  7. Monitor, amend, renew or terminate
  8. Close obligations, deposits and archive records

Production safeguards

  • Use least-privilege access; restrict legal, employment, lease and dispute documents to need-to-know users.
  • Store signed files in private encrypted object storage; malware-scan uploads and maintain version history.
  • Record counterparty legal identity, GST/tax details where applicable, signatories and verification.
  • Require legal review for leases, employment, high-value, regulated, data-processing and dispute-sensitive agreements.
  • Use effective-dated approval limits and signatory delegations; requester cannot approve own request.
  • Keep immutable audit events for viewing, upload, download, approval, amendment, renewal and termination.
  • Send expiry alerts with delivery logs; provide escalation and backup contract owner.
  • Do not auto-renew or terminate solely from a reminder; require authorised human decision.
