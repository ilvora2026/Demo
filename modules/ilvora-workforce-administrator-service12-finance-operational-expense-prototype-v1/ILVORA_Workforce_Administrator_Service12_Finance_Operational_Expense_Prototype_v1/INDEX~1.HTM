Budget, Payments & Financial Summary

Budget allocation

Track annual and monthly budget by branch and category, approved by the designated financial authority. Compare actual, committed and remaining budget.

Payment tracking

Record payment date, payment method, transaction reference, amount paid and outstanding balance only after approval and payment evidence.

Recurring expenses

Maintain rent, utilities, subscriptions and service contracts with renewal dates, due dates and responsible owner.

Management reports

Branch-wise, category-wise, monthly and annual summaries; pending liabilities; budget variance; approved and paid totals.
