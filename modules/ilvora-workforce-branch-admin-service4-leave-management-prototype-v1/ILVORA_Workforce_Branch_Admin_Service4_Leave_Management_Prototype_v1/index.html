Review and approval routing

This prototype simulates Branch Admin/HR review and routing. Head Office decisions are represented for demonstration; in production they must be completed by an authorised Head Office user.
Authority rule: paid medical leave is always routed to Head Office. Branch Admin/HR cannot approve it, even when within the annual five-day paid allowance. Leave exceeding five days is routed to Head Office. Branch Admin/HR may approve casual leave up to five days under the configured policy.

Paid medical leave is limited to five approved days per employee per year. Head Office must explicitly approve the paid status. If the allowance is exhausted, further medical leave is unpaid unless a revised policy is formally approved.

Payroll and attendance integration

  • Only final approved leave records update attendance.
  • Approved paid medical leave is treated as paid absence within the annual allowance.
  • Approved unpaid medical and casual leave are passed to payroll as unpaid leave according to the configured salary rules.
  • Rejected or pending leave does not change attendance or payroll.
  • Every approval, rejection, return and payroll hand-off must be audited.
