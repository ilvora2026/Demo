Approval Rules & Role Boundaries

Procurement authority must be configured and enforced server-side.

Proposed responsibility matrix

Important: No numerical procurement approval limit has been supplied yet. Until the Managing Director formally sets an effective-dated limit, all purchase commitments must route to the Managing Director. Related purchases must not be split to circumvent the limit. Requester and approver must be different people.

Required controls before production

  • Three-quote policy or documented exception for urgent/single-source purchases.
  • Vendor due diligence, GST and bank-account verification; independent bank-detail change checks.
  • Budget availability and purchase-limit checks before PO issuance.
  • Goods receipt and quality check by an authorised person independent of the requester where practical.
  • Stock adjustment reason, dual approval for material adjustments and complete audit trail.
  • Asset tagging, custody assignment, transfer acknowledgement and disposal approval.
  • Conflict-of-interest declaration and prohibition on order splitting.
