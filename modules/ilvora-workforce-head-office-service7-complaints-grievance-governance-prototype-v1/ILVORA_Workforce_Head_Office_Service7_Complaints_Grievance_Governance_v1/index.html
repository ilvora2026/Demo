Complaint narratives and evidence are restricted to independently authorised case personnel.

Confidentiality safeguards

Conflict of interest: a person named in a complaint must not view, investigate, assign, approve or close it.
Complaints naming Branch Admin/HR bypass that person and route to an independent Head Office officer.
Complaints involving Head Office HR or senior management go outside the implicated reporting line to an independent authority or external channel.
Head Office users do not receive blanket access to all confidential complaints.

Case investigation workspace

Investigation rules

  • Investigator must be independent of all persons named.
  • Record factual, dated and attributable notes.
  • Restrict evidence to assigned authorised officers.
  • Record information requests and response dates.
  • Closure requires an independent reviewer not implicated in the complaint.
  • Disclose only appropriate status updates to the employee.
Production requires secure attachment storage, server-side role checks and immutable audit events.

Escalation routing

Branch Admin / HR named: exclude them and route to independent HO grievance authority.
Head Office HR / senior management named: route outside the implicated reporting line to an independent authority or external channel.
If an investigator becomes conflicted, reassign immediately and retain prior assignment history.
Only an independent authorised reviewer may approve closure.

Grievance reports

Choose a report and generate it. Aggregate reports exclude sensitive complaint narratives and evidence.

Reporting safeguards

  • Branch reports show only information the recipient is authorised to see.
  • Aggregate analytics minimise disclosure of complainant identity.
  • Do not include sensitive narratives or evidence in general dashboards.
  • Log report generation and recipients where required.

Case audit history

Retention and records controls

  • Use a formally approved HR/legal retention schedule for complaint records, evidence and outcomes.
  • Legal, audit, investigation and regulatory holds pause deletion.
  • Maintain secure physical records and print/filing register where required.
  • Use encryption, access logs, least-privilege access and secure evidence storage.
  • Define secure destruction after authorised retention expiry.
Do not apply a generic automatic deletion period to grievance files without HR/legal approval. Holds must prevent deletion.
Production must preserve immutable access, assignment, reassignment, decision and closure history.
