ILVORA WORKFORCE
Head Office Admin Panel – Service 7: Complaints & Grievance Governance
Prototype v1

RUN
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Explore Central Register, Investigation, Independent Escalation, Reports, and Audit & Retention.
4. Select a sample case and try updating its investigator, status, notes or outcome.
5. Generate a branch-wise or consolidated report and export the register as CSV.

INCLUDED
- Central complaint register for Head Office – Kochi, Neelambari Hospital – Thamarakulam and ILVORA Health Clinic – Kottarakkara.
- Branch, status and priority filters.
- Conflict-of-interest flags and independent escalation queue.
- Investigation assignment, notes, status, outcome and evidence-reference fields.
- Independent routing for complaints naming Branch Admin/HR or senior management.
- Aggregate reports, CSV export, print/Save as PDF, audit history and retention safeguards.

GOVERNANCE
- A person named in a complaint must not access, investigate, assign, approve or close that complaint.
- Complaints naming Branch Admin/HR bypass that person and route to an independent authorised Head Office officer.
- Complaints involving Head Office HR or senior management route outside the implicated reporting line to an independent authority or external channel.
- Closure requires an independent authorised reviewer.
- General dashboards and aggregate reports should not expose sensitive narratives or evidence.

LIMITATIONS
Front-end demo only. It is not connected to Cloudflare D1, GitHub, live employee records, authentication, real role enforcement, secure attachment storage, notifications or external escalation channels. All records are illustrative browser data and may reset on refresh.
Do not enter real employee names, allegations, medical details or sensitive evidence. Production requires secure case-level permissions, encrypted evidence storage, immutable audit logs, anti-retaliation safeguards, independent reporting channels, defined response timelines and an HR/legal-approved retention schedule.
