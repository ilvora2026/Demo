Leave policy summary

  • Medical leave: up to five days per year are eligible for payment, subject to approval and applicable documentation rules.
  • Medical leave beyond the five paid days is unpaid, subject to approval.
  • Casual leave is unpaid.
  • Requests of up to five days are routed to Branch Admin / HR. Requests exceeding five days are routed to Head Office for approval.
This prototype reflects the policy details supplied for the project. Annual entitlement, leave-year dates, half-day rules, supporting-document thresholds and any statutory overrides must be confirmed before production.
