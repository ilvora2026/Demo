# ILVORA Workforce — Employee Service 3: My Leave

## Policy rules represented in this prototype
- Medical leave: up to 5 approved days per year are eligible for payment; additional medical leave is unpaid.
- Casual leave: unpaid.
- Requests up to 5 days: Branch Admin / HR approval.
- Requests exceeding 5 days: Head Office approval.
- Leave approval and payment eligibility are separate decisions.

## Prototype features
- Leave application form
- Leave type and indicative payment treatment
- Duration-based approval routing display
- Employee leave request history
- Leave policy summary

## Important limitations
This is a front-end demo with sample data. It does not connect to a real database, authentication, payroll, notifications, document upload or approval workflow. Paid-leave balance is illustrative and must be calculated from approved records in the live system. Annual leave-year dates, entitlement, half-day rules, medical certificate thresholds, holiday/weekend counting and statutory overrides require policy confirmation.
