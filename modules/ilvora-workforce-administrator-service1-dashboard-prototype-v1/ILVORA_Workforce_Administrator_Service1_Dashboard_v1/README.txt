ILVORA WORKFORCE
Administrator Role – Service 1: Administrator Dashboard
Prototype v1

RUN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Explore Overview, Employee Administration, Payroll Control, Account Control and System & Audit tabs.
4. Use the sample Review buttons in Account Control to see the demo confirmation.

INCLUDED
- Administrator dashboard overview across the three branches.
- Workforce and payroll status indicators.
- Pending salary, payslip and account action summary.
- Employee administration responsibility overview.
- Payroll control and governance summary.
- Account access oversight and termination safeguards.
- System role boundaries and audit/security guidance.
- Responsive layout and print/Save PDF.

ROLE BOUNDARIES
- Administrator: salary master, payroll processing, payslip authorisation and approved account deactivation controls.
- Head Office Admin: branch management, employee onboarding approval and Branch Admin / HR authorisation.
- Managing Director: executive oversight and separately authorised strategic reporting.
- Branch Admin / HR: branch-scoped operational HR and attendance workflows.

LIMITATIONS
Front-end prototype only. Sample figures and employee references are illustrative. No live authentication, Cloudflare D1 integration, salary database, payroll calculation, bank transfer, payslip generation, account revocation or real permission enforcement is connected. Buttons labelled planned/demo do not execute production actions.
Do not enter real employee personal, salary, bank or other sensitive information.

PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS
- Enforce permissions server-side with least privilege.
- Protect salary and payroll records with encryption, access logging and approval workflows.
- Require effective dates and audit history for salary changes.
- Reconcile attendance and approved leave before payroll.
- Handle post-payroll corrections through adjustment entries.
- Use secure payslip delivery and authenticated expiring downloads.
- Revoke access based on approved HR events and effective dates without deleting employee records.
- Maintain immutable audit logs and legal/HR-approved retention schedule.
