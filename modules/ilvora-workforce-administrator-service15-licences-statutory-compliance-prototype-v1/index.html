Authority, Access & Regulatory Guidance

Define who maintains, verifies, approves and escalates compliance records.

Responsibility matrix

RolePermitted actionsRestrictions

Potential regulatory areas to assess (not a legal determination)

  • Clinical establishment / facility registration and applicable state requirements.
  • Drug and pharmacy licences, pharmacist requirements and applicable drug-control conditions.
  • Fire safety, building occupancy, electrical safety and local body approvals.
  • Biomedical waste authorisation, agreements, records and applicable pollution-control requirements.
  • Laboratory, imaging, blood storage or other specialised approvals where those services are actually provided.
  • Professional registration and credential validity for regulated staff.
  • Insurance coverage, policy conditions and renewal.
Important: Applicability, issuing authority, validity and renewal process depend on facility type, services, location, ownership, capacity and current law. Have a qualified Kerala healthcare compliance professional verify the actual requirement list for each branch. This prototype is not a legal compliance checklist or legal advice.

Production safeguards

  • Use server-side role-based access and least privilege for sensitive licences and personal credentials.
  • Private encrypted document storage, malware scanning, version history and audit of every view/download/change.
  • Verify certificate authenticity and scope; record issuing authority, premises, conditions and responsible person.
  • Use effective-dated reminders, delivery logs, escalation and substitute owners.
  • Never auto-renew, self-certify or assume compliance solely from an uploaded file.
  • Preserve original documents and regulator communications under a formally approved retention schedule.
