ILVORA Workforce — Administrator Service 15
Licences, Registrations & Statutory Compliance
Version 1 — Front-end demonstration

RUN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or another modern browser.

FEATURES
- Central licence, registration and certificate register
- Branch-specific and central compliance records
- Categories for clinical/facility, pharmacy/drug, fire/building, pollution/biomedical waste, laboratory/diagnostic, professional registration, insurance and other approvals
- Issuing authority, reference number, issue/expiry dates, responsible owner and evidence reference
- Renewal calendar and 180/90/60/30-day reminder concept
- Inspection and regulatory visit register
- Corrective-action tracking and closure evidence concept
- Branch-wise and consolidated reports, CSV export and print/Save PDF
- Role boundaries and production safeguards

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
This is a front-end prototype with illustrative sample records and dates. The examples do not establish that any ILVORA branch holds, requires, or is non-compliant with a particular licence. The prototype is not connected to Cloudflare D1, GitHub, authentication, email/SMS, regulator portals or document storage. It stores document filenames/references only. No renewal application, fee payment, regulatory filing, inspection booking or approval is actually submitted.

REGULATORY CAUTION
Applicability and validity depend on the facility, services, location, capacity, ownership and current law. The potential regulatory areas shown are prompts for a qualified Kerala healthcare compliance professional to assess—not a definitive legal checklist or legal advice. Confirm each requirement, issuing authority, conditions, renewal period and responsible qualified person directly with the relevant regulator and legal adviser.

ROLE GOVERNANCE
- Branch Admin/HR submits branch evidence and reports deadlines.
- Head Office Admin verifies document completeness.
- Administrator maintains the register, assigns owners and coordinates renewals.
- Managing Director oversees critical risks and approves major compliance expenditure.
- Uploading a certificate does not itself establish legal compliance. Do not mark expired or unverified records as current.

PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS
Implement server-side RBAC, private encrypted file storage, malware scanning, authenticity/expiry checks, immutable audit logs, scheduled notifications with delivery evidence, escalation and backup owners, corrective-action verification, legal holds, and a formally approved India/Kerala statutory retention schedule. Do not auto-renew or submit regulatory filings without authorised human review.
