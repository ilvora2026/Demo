IDs are permanent, unique, branch-specific and never reused. Generate only after final Head Office approval.

Approval checklist

  • Mandatory identity and contact fields completed
  • PAN may be left blank
  • Up to 7 image/PDF documents reviewed
  • Branch and department confirmed
  • Duplicate identity / employee check completed
  • Decision and approver recorded
Workflow: Branch Admin submits onboarding → Head Office verifies credentials → approval triggers permanent Employee ID generation → activation instructions are initiated for the employee. Returned or rejected applications do not receive an Employee ID. Salary remains Administrator-controlled and is not editable in this service.
Prototype limitation: This demo uses sample records and browser-only actions. ID allocation is simulated; production must use a server-side atomic unique-ID allocator, secure document storage, authenticated permissions, duplicate checks, immutable audit logs and real notification delivery.
