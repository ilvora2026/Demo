Attendance information

  • Employees can view their own attendance and assigned shifts.
  • Employees cannot directly edit verified attendance punches.
  • Corrections are submitted to the authorised Branch Admin / HR workflow.
  • Approved changes must preserve the original record and an audit trail.
  • Attendance Marker records are linked to the employee's unique branch ID.
The exact grace periods, early/late calculation, checkout extension, QR/GPS validation and photo-retention rules will be implemented only after the attendance policy is confirmed for this module.
