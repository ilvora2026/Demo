Digital retention schedule

  • Detailed roster: retain until the 15th of the following month, then eligible for deletion under the approved schedule.
  • Brief summary: retain for 3 years.
  • Reminder: notify custodians before the deletion date.
  • Legal hold: pause deletion for audit, investigation, litigation or regulatory requirements.
Printing does not itself authorise digital deletion. Physical printed records follow a separate approved retention and secure-destruction schedule.
