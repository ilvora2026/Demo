Production controls

  • Read only from approved, locked payroll snapshots.
  • Bank payment status must be supported by verified bank evidence.
  • Use maker-checker approval for payment files and reconciliation exceptions.
  • Mask bank account details and restrict exports.
  • Maintain immutable logs of report generation, exports and reconciliation decisions.
  • Encrypt payroll and payment data in transit and at rest.
  • Apply retention, legal hold and physical print/filing register requirements.
  • Do not treat a generated bank file as proof of payment.
