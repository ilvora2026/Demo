ILVORA Workforce – Administrator Service 6
Payroll Reports & Financial Reconciliation Prototype v1

RUN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. Explore Financial Overview, Branch Reports, Payment Register, Bank Reconciliation,
   Arrears & Adjustments, Reports & Audit History, and Access Controls.

FEATURES
- Monthly payroll expenditure summary.
- Branch and department payroll reports.
- Gross, adjustments, deductions and net-pay summaries.
- Illustrative bank payment register and payment status.
- Reconciliation workspace with matched/unmatched totals.
- Arrears and adjustment register.
- CSV exports and print / Save as PDF through browser.
- Saved report history and audit-history concept.
- Role-based report access and production controls.

LIMITATIONS
- Front-end demo only. All employee IDs, names, amounts and bank references are fictional.
- No bank API, payment initiation, accounting integration, payroll connector, real statement
  import, backend, authentication or enforced permissions.
- Demo actions are local browser interactions and reset on refresh.
- Do not enter real employee, bank, salary or financial information.
- Marking a payment matched in this demo does not verify a real bank credit.

PRODUCTION CONTROLS
- Read only from approved and locked payroll snapshots.
- Reconcile using verified bank statement imports/API and evidence.
- Use maker-checker approval for payment files and exceptions.
- Mask bank account details and restrict exports.
- Log report views, exports, payment changes and reconciliation decisions.
- Encrypt data in transit and at rest; apply retention and legal-hold controls.
- Maintain required physical print/filing register; printing does not authorise digital deletion.
