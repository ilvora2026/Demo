Mandatory production safeguards

  • Verify approved termination or resignation documentation and effective date.
  • Resignation: disable login at the end of the approved last working day.
  • Termination: disable at the approved effective date/time.
  • Revoke active sessions, refresh tokens and remembered devices.
  • Require reason, reference, actor, timestamp and independent approval trail.
  • Preserve all employment and statutory records under approved retention schedule.
  • Use server-side RBAC, MFA for privileged users and immutable audit logs.
  • Provide a controlled appeal/correction process for mistaken deactivation.
