Central oversight

Urgent queue: prioritise safety, access and payroll-impacting issues under approved service timelines.
Requests should be assigned to a named team or officer with a target response and resolution date.
Branch-level tickets remain visible to the originating branch only where appropriate; central access follows delegated authority.
Escalation does not remove the original ticket history or accountability of the assigned team.

Request management workspace

Service handling standards

  • Every ticket receives a unique reference and employee ID link.
  • Assign a responsible team/officer and target date.
  • Record each progress update and status change.
  • Escalate unresolved, overdue or high-risk tickets.
  • Record resolution and any employee feedback.
  • Close only after authorised review.
Production SLA targets must be defined by category and priority; demo due dates are not live service commitments.

Escalation rules

Escalate urgent safety or serious operational incidents immediately to the designated authority.
Escalate overdue tickets according to approved service timelines.
Escalate unresolved requests when the assigned team cannot resolve them within its authority.
Retain original assignment, notes and escalation trail.

Helpdesk reports

Choose a report and generate it.

Reporting controls

  • Branch reports follow role-based access permissions.
  • Consolidated reports may be accessed by authorised central roles.
  • Do not expose sensitive employee or attachment details unnecessarily.
  • Record report generation and export events in production.

Request audit history

Retention and records controls

  • Retain ticket, assignment, communication, resolution and approval history under an HR/legal-approved records schedule.
  • Apply legal, audit, investigation and regulatory holds before deletion.
  • Keep required physical print records with a print/filing register and secure storage.
  • Restrict attachments to authorised users and use secure storage.
  • Define secure destruction after approved retention expiry.
Do not delete a ticket simply because it is marked resolved. Retention must follow the approved records schedule.
Production must maintain immutable status, assignment, escalation and closure audit events.
