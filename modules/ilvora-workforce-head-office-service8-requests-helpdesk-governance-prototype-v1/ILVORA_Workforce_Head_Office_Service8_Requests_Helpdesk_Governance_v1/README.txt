ILVORA WORKFORCE
Head Office Admin Panel – Service 8: Requests & Helpdesk Governance
Prototype v1

RUN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Explore the Central Request Queue, Request Management, Escalations, Reports, and Audit & Retention tabs.
4. Select a sample request to assign an owner, change priority/status, add progress notes, set a target date, record resolution or escalate.
5. Generate reports and export the sample register as CSV.

INCLUDED
- Central all-branch request register.
- Branch, status and priority filters.
- Request assignment to HR, IT, payroll, facilities, branch HR and administration teams.
- Priority, target date, progress notes and resolution tracking.
- Higher-level escalation queue.
- Branch-wise and consolidated reports.
- CSV export, print/Save as PDF, audit history and retention safeguards.

BRANCHES
- Head Office – Kochi
- Neelambari Hospital – Thamarakulam
- ILVORA Health Clinic – Kottarakkara

LIMITATIONS
Front-end demo only. Not connected to Cloudflare D1, GitHub, live employee accounts, authentication, real role permissions, notifications, email/SMS, SLA timers or secure attachment storage. All data is illustrative and may reset on refresh.
Production requires server-side access controls, unique ticket IDs, secure attachments, defined category/priority SLAs, escalation rules, immutable audit logs, notification integrations, approved retention schedule and legal holds.
Do not enter real employee personal or sensitive information into this demo.
