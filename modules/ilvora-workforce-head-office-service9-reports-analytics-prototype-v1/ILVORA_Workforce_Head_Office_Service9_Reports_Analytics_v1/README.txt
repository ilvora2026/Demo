ILVORA WORKFORCE
Head Office Admin Panel – Service 9: Reports & Analytics
Prototype v1

RUN
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Use the tabs to review executive, workforce, attendance, leave, roster, payroll-status, compliance, complaints aggregate and helpdesk reports.
4. Select branch and reporting period filters.
5. In Report Centre, select a report and branch scope, then generate, print or export CSV.

INCLUDED
- Consolidated overview for Head Office – Kochi, Neelambari Hospital – Thamarakulam and ILVORA Health Clinic – Kottarakkara.
- Employee strength and department summaries.
- Attendance, absenteeism and exception indicators.
- Leave utilisation and roster coverage.
- Restricted aggregate payroll status (read-only; no individual salary or bank details).
- Onboarding, document verification and policy acknowledgement indicators.
- Aggregate complaint/grievance and helpdesk performance reporting.
- Branch and consolidated report generation, print/Save PDF and CSV export.
- Access, audit and retention safeguards.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
This is a front-end prototype with illustrative sample figures only. It is not connected to Cloudflare D1, a live HR database, attendance devices, payroll, employee accounts, complaints or helpdesk systems. Filters and reports operate on sample data only. It does not provide real authentication, enforce role permissions, or send notifications.
Do not enter real employee, salary, medical, bank, grievance or other sensitive information.

PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS
- Secure server-side role and branch-level access controls.
- Live, validated source data and consistent reporting definitions.
- Audit logging for report views, filters, exports and print actions.
- Privacy-minimised aggregate dashboards; restricted case-level complaint access.
- Payroll summaries read-only except authorised Administrator workflows.
- Approved HR/legal retention schedule, print/filing register, secure storage and legal holds.
- Reconciliation, data-quality checks and report-generation timestamps.
