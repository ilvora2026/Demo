Authority & Safeguards

Administrator

Register case; ensure notices and employee response are documented; assign investigator; monitor timelines; record findings; submit recommended action to the Managing Director. Cannot finally approve or implement a disciplinary sanction.

Managing Director

Sole final decision-maker for all disciplinary actions, including warning, suspension, demotion, financial penalty where lawful, and termination. Must record decision, reasons and effective date.

Branch Admin / HR

May report incidents and provide records or factual verification. No authority to decide or impose disciplinary action.

Employee rights

Employee must receive appropriate notice, an opportunity to respond, access to appeal/review as per policy, and protection from retaliation. Maintain confidentiality and impartiality.

Production controls: restrict case access to authorised persons; any person named in a complaint or conflict must be excluded from access, investigation and decision-making; encrypt evidence; keep an immutable audit trail; define appeal reviewer independent of the original decision-maker where practicable; follow applicable employment law and approved HR policy.
