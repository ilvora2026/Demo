ILVORA Workforce – Administrator Service 8
Employee Compliance & Disciplinary Management – Prototype v1

Open index.html in a modern browser.

Approved governance:
- Administrator manages case workflow and submits recommendations.
- Managing Director has sole final approval authority for ALL disciplinary actions, including warnings, suspension and termination.
- No disciplinary sanction is effective before the Managing Director's recorded decision.
- Branch Admin/HR may report incidents and provide factual records, but cannot decide or impose sanctions.

Prototype limitations:
- Front-end demonstration only; fictional sample data.
- No authentication, server-side role enforcement, database persistence, secure evidence storage, real notifications or immutable audit log.
- The MD decision panel is illustrative and is not protected by actual login permissions.
- Changes are in-memory and reset on refresh.

Production requirements:
- Enforce MD-only final approval server-side for every disciplinary action.
- Maintain fair process: notice, employee response, impartial investigation, reasoned decision and appeal/review.
- Exclude conflicted persons from access, investigation and decisions.
- Encrypt and tightly restrict case evidence; keep immutable timestamps and audit history.
- Define lawful disciplinary measures, timelines, appeal process and retention under applicable employment law and approved HR policy.
